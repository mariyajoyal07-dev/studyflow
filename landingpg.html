About ProSkills

One search, five accredited partners

ProSkills brings courses from multiple accredited partners into one convenient platform, helping learners discover the right qualification without visiting each accreditation website individually. Every course still lives — and is delivered, assessed and certified — on its own accreditation partner's site; ProSkills is the discovery layer that helps you find it.
